# Botbench: product one-pager

**What it is:** Plan LEGO 51515 and EV3 31313 robot builds, rehearse them in a 2D simulator and export Pybricks code, up to behaviours picked by Jev

**Who it is for:** someone who owns a Robot Inventor or EV3 box (or both), has built the box models, and wants the robot to do something of their own without guessing which ports, sensors and firmware that takes.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** at a desk next to a half-built rover, laptop open, deciding where the colour sensor goes and tuning a line follower's gain before the batteries are even in.

**What done looks like:** a build that validates against the parts in the box, a brain that reaches the goal in the arena, and a `.py` file ready to flash.
